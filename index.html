Red Hat Quay 3.18 Workshop

Introduction

Red Hat Quay is a container image registry that provides secure and reliable storage, distribution, and management of container images. It’s built for enterprise environments, offering scalability, automation, and seamless integration with OpenShift.

This is not an official guide or workshop provided by Red Hat. For official training, documentation, or guidance related to this product, please contact your designated Red Hat representative.
